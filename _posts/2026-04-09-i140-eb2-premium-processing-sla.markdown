If you're going through the US employment-based green card process, understanding the I-140 premium processing timeline is critical for planning. Here's a breakdown of the EB-2 I-140 premium processing SLA based on current USCIS guidelines.

<!--more-->

## What is the I-140?

Form I-140, Immigrant Petition for Alien Workers, is the petition your employer files with USCIS to sponsor you for a green card. For EB-2, this covers professionals with advanced degrees (Master's or higher) or individuals with exceptional ability in their field.

## Premium Processing (Form I-907)

Premium processing is an optional expedited service offered by USCIS. By filing Form I-907 alongside the I-140, your employer requests that USCIS take action on the petition within a guaranteed timeframe.

### Current SLA

- **Processing guarantee:** USCIS will issue an initial action within **45 business days** of receiving the I-907 request.
- **Premium processing fee:** **$2,805** (check the USCIS website for the latest fee, as it is subject to change).

"Initial action" means USCIS will do one of the following within the 45 business day window:

1. **Approve** the petition
2. **Deny** the petition
3. **Issue a Request for Evidence (RFE)**
4. **Issue a Notice of Intent to Deny (NOID)**
5. **Open an investigation** for fraud or misrepresentation

If USCIS issues an RFE, the 45 business day clock resets once the response is received.

### If USCIS Misses the Deadline

If USCIS fails to take action within 45 business days, you are entitled to a refund of the premium processing fee. The underlying I-140 petition continues to be processed. You may also re-file Form I-907 to restart the premium processing clock.

## Regular vs. Premium Processing

| | Regular Processing | Premium Processing |
|---|---|---|
| **Timeline** | Varies (typically 6-12+ months) | 45 business days |
| **Additional Fee** | None | $2,805 |
| **Form** | I-140 only | I-140 + I-907 |

## Tips

- **Check processing times regularly.** USCIS publishes estimated processing times on their website. Regular processing times fluctuate significantly.
- **Premium processing can be filed with the I-140 or after.** You can upgrade an already-pending I-140 to premium processing at any time.
- **An RFE is not a denial.** If you receive an RFE, work with your immigration attorney to respond thoroughly within the deadline.
- **Keep copies of everything.** Maintain records of all receipt notices, filing dates, and correspondence.

## Important Caveats

Immigration policies and fees change frequently.
